The Firm

Human led. AI powered.

Integryl is a specialist advisory firm for financial institutions, focused on artificial intelligence, cybersecurity and risk.

Our purpose

Institutions rarely need more technology. They need sound judgment about it.

Financial institutions are expected to defend against more capable adversaries, meet rising supervisory expectations and adopt artificial intelligence, often at the same time and with the same team.

Integryl was founded to provide senior, practical support for that work. We test, we advise and, where it serves the institution, we assume the leadership role and own the program.

Our focus is financial institutions. We also advise organizations in other regulated sectors where the same disciplines apply.

We also publish our thinking. Integryl Intelligence is issued every business morning, so our perspective is on the record before any engagement begins.
